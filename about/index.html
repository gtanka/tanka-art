About

Tanka Art is a personal collection of handmade jewelry, drawings, and paintings, created in Tel Aviv.

I enjoy making beautiful things and exploring the relationships between shapes, colors, textures, and materials.

My work is inspired by the beauty of natural stones, simple forms, unexpected combinations, and the small details that make each piece unique.

Tanka Art is a space where I bring together the things I make, experiment with, and love.
